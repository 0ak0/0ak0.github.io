Myself

I'm Oaken, a first class Game Design graduate, based in the UK. While knowledgeable in programming, my main interest and skillset is in the design work for virtual environments, such as level, UI, prop, and lighting design.

My work includes not just creating games, but adjusting them and their environments to properly adapt to what the player's needs are, such as in VR and XR applications. I also do simple 2D work, intended for use in texturing, logos, and UI elements.

Level Design

My work in Level and Virtual Environment Design includes not only creating levels and environments, but also modelling work and lighting.

I have experience in creating environments tailored for XR use cases, taking into consideration the needs and requirements of such platforms. VR applications especially bring their own concerns for level design, for both getting a player's attention and the requirements of more diegetic elements.

Concerning lighting, I use mixtures of diegetic and non-diegetic lights to correctly light scenes as needed and also capture the player's attention when required. I create scenes and environments with the goal of drawing player attention naturally and without the need for overly obvious markers or signals.

2D Design

My work 2D design mostly revolves around UI work and Vector Illustrations. My "Oak Leaf" logo is one such example. I have created UI elements for both games and standard applications, as well as helping to design and redesign existing UI for existing applications.

For vector illustrations, I have mainly worked on logos in the past, but have also done work on more creative tasks such as desktop wallpapers and banners for games and their communities.
